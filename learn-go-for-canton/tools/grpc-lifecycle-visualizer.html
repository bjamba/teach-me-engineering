gRPC Call Lifecycle

Step through the four gRPC method types — unary, server-streaming, client-streaming, bidirectional — and see exactly which messages cross the wire and in what order.

What you're looking at

Two timelines (client and server), time flowing top-to-bottom. Each labeled arrow is a message on the wire. Server-streaming and bidirectional methods hold the channel open across multiple messages.

The thing to internalize: the call itself is one HTTP/2 stream. Multiple messages on a streaming RPC are framed inside that single stream. Cancellation tears down the whole thing — including any in-flight Send/Recv on either side.
