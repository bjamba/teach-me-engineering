Goroutine & Channel Simulator

Step through several canonical goroutine + channel scenarios. Watch goroutines block, unblock, and exit. Each scenario shows the equivalent Go code so you can map the picture to the syntax.

What you're looking at

  • Each circle is a goroutine. Color = state. Green = running, blue = ready, orange = blocked, gray = exited.
  • Each rectangle with internal slots is a channel. Slots fill (sends) and empty (receives).
  • Arrows show send/receive direction at the moment they fire.
  • The event log below shows what just happened. Useful when something blocks and you can't tell why visually.
