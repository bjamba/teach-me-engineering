Your DSA notebook

Every note you've captured from a 📓 button — across lessons and drills — collected here, grouped by module, ready to export. The Markdown export is the file you keep on your computer or cloud drive as your personal deep-dive reference.

How to use this

1. Open any lesson or drill. Tap the 📓 floating button.

2. Capture the metaphor that finally made it click. The trade-off you didn't see at first. The wrong answer you gave and what fixed it. The "I'd say X if asked in an interview" rehearsal line.

3. When you've got enough, hit Download Markdown. That file is yours forever — drop it into Notion, Obsidian, a Google Doc, or just a folder. It's organized by module so it ages well.
