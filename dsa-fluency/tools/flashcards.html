How spaced repetition works here

Each card has a level 0–5. Reviewing correctly bumps it up; "Again" knocks it to 0. Higher levels wait longer to come back: level 1 = next session, level 5 = ~16 sessions away. The point isn't to finish — it's to keep the interval matching how solid each fact is.
