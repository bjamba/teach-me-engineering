Why this matters

A too-vowel-heavy deck means easy short words but low scores. A too-consonant-heavy deck means frequent dead hands. Scrabble's distribution is the result of decades of tuning. Use this tool to understand it, then decide if Lexicon Duel needs to tweak it (e.g. slightly more vowels for faster pacing).
