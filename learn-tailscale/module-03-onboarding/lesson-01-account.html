Create your account & tailnet

Five minutes. After this, you have a tailnet (still empty), and you can start adding devices.

1Sign up

  1. Open login.tailscale.com/start in your browser.
  2. Pick an identity provider — Google, Microsoft, GitHub, or Apple. Use the email you'll always have access to. Switching providers later is a hassle.
  3. Authorize Tailscale.
  4. You're in. The admin console at login.tailscale.com/admin is now your tailnet's home.

Free tier covers everything in this course: 100 devices, 3 users, every feature. No credit card required.

2Tour the admin console

Take a minute to look around. The pages you'll come back to:

  • Machines — your devices. Empty right now. We'll fill it in starting next lesson.
  • Users — people in your tailnet. Just you so far.
  • Access controls — your ACL policy in HuJSON. Don't touch yet (M4 covers this).
  • DNS — DNS settings for your tailnet, including the MagicDNS toggle.
  • Settings → Auth keys — pre-shared keys for headless installs (M3L6).

3Turn on MagicDNS

Almost everything in this course assumes MagicDNS is on. To enable it:

  1. Go to DNS in the admin console.
  2. Find the MagicDNS section. Click Enable MagicDNS.
  3. Confirm the prompt. Done.

You'll also see your tailnet's domain (something like tail-abc123.ts.net). You can rename it under DNS → Tailnet name if you want something prettier (e.g. your-tailnet.ts.net). This is purely cosmetic.

4Pick which device to add first

You're ready. Open the lesson for whichever OS you have on the machine you're sitting at right now:

  • L2 · macOS
  • L3 · Windows
  • L4 · Linux
  • L5 · iOS & Android
  • L6 · Headless server / Raspberry Pi

Add one or two devices, then jump to L7 · Verifying it works to confirm the tailnet is alive.
