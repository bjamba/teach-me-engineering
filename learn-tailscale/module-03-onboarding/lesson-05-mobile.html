iOS & Android

Two apps, one for each. Both work the same way.

1Install

  • iOS / iPadOS: App Store → Tailscale
  • Android: Play Store → Tailscale (or F-Droid for the Google-free version)

2Log in

  1. Open the app.
  2. Tap Get Started → choose your identity provider (the same one as L1).
  3. The app opens a browser, you authorize, you're back in the app, connected.
  4. iOS will ask permission to add a VPN profile. Tap Allow. (Android does the equivalent.) This is required — Tailscale uses the OS's VPN framework to route traffic.

Note for iOS users: the "VPN" notification icon in the status bar is normal and expected. It just means a tunnel is active.

3Toggle on/off

The app has one big switch. Off = your phone behaves normally. On = you have access to your tailnet AND your phone shows up to other tailnet devices.

Battery cost is negligible. You can leave it on permanently. Most people do.

4Try it

From your phone's browser, navigate to http://home-server (using your actual server's hostname). If you have a service running there, it loads — straight from your phone, anywhere in the world.

If nothing's running yet, that's fine. The connectivity will be there when you start hosting things in M4 onwards.

5What's next

Add any remaining devices. If you have a server, head to L6.
